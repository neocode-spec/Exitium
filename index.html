THE PRODUCT

Meet Exitium Nano.

A desktop AI experience designed to bring useful intelligence closer to your own computer.

Your computer.
Your assistant.
Your control.

Exitium Nano is a personal Windows desktop AI project focused on local conversations, conversation memory and useful everyday assistance.

The goal is to make AI more accessible without requiring every interaction to depend on a remote AI service. Online features, when used, may still require an internet connection.

BUILT WITH PURPOSE

More than another
AI interface.

The principles behind Exitium focus on independence, usability and giving people more control over how they use AI.

◉

Local-first AI

Designed to run supported AI models locally, reducing dependence on cloud inference for supported tasks.

⌘

Conversation memory

Retain useful conversation context so your assistant can build on earlier interactions instead of treating every chat as entirely new.

⌕

Optional web search

Search beyond the local model when online information is needed. Web searches send queries to an external search service.

⌘

Practical assistance

Ask questions, explore ideas, work through problems and get assistance with supported computer-based tasks.

◌

Privacy-conscious design

Local processing can keep supported prompts on your device. Connected services may transmit data when you use them.

↗

Designed to evolve

Improvements are guided by practical needs, real testing and verified stability rather than promises about unfinished features.

THE THINKING

Technology should
work for you.

Exitium is an independent technology project exploring a more personal approach to artificial intelligence. It begins with desktop software and a straightforward idea: useful technology should be practical, understandable and built with its users in mind.

The project prioritises local operation where possible, honest product information and improvements driven by testing rather than exaggerated promises.

Privacy matters
